# The R3 SWEEP — the complement audit over the whole recorded corpus

2026-09-16, with Mato. The MEASUREMENT PHASE of step 5 in
[`plan-divergent-matching.md`](plan-divergent-matching.md), and the first real use of
`skills/refdiff/reconcile.md` §R3 — read-only, over all 52 recorded pairs, no capture and no
server.

**What it was for.** Step 5 wants to refuse pairs on weak evidence, and named CONTAINMENT as the
discriminator. That claim rested on **7 labelled text pairs out of 1424**, three of which it was
derived from — illustrated, not validated. This builds a labelled set large enough to FALSIFY a
rule, and then falsifies one.

**Headline: the plan's proposed discriminator is refuted, and a different defect was found.**
Containment does not separate the populations in any of the three readings its own wording allows.
Neither does distance, in either axis. What does separate — and what nothing in the plan had
looked at — is the **slot pass**, which is width-blind by design and was width-blind without a
bound.

---

## Method, and what makes it admissible

Every run dir's `elements.json` holds both **aligned** leaf sets and `matchElements` is exported
from `dist`, so the shipped matcher can be re-run over any recorded pair. The gate is that the
recomputation must reproduce that pair's recorded `matching` block exactly — matched,
`matchedVia.{text,slot,geometry}`, `designOnly`, `implOnly` — or the pair is dropped, because a
reconstruction that does not reproduce the run is not evidence about it.

**52 run dirs read, 52 admissible, 0 dropped.** The text-γ distribution reproduces the previous
session's figures to the decimal (p50 27.9, p90 156.5, p99 544.0, max 1261.1 over 1424 text
pairs), which is the independent check that this is the same matcher on the same inputs.

Two candidate selectors, never mixed, because the second family is invisible to the first:

| selector | asks | candidates |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--gamma 200` | what did the matcher claim from FAR AWAY | 92 |
| `--slot-ratio 4` | what did the width-blind pass claim that is not the same SIZE of thing | 15 |

`slotGamma` caps a slot pair's POSITION distance at 40 px by construction, so a slot mis-pairing is
always nearby — the γ selector cannot see the family at all, and 8 of the 15 only appear in the
first list because their full γ carries the width term `slotGamma` deliberately drops.

**Scripts** (all read-only with respect to every run dir):

```bash
node scripts/r3-sweep.ts --gamma 200 --detail --out <out>.md   # the candidate table + evidence
node scripts/r3-sweep.ts --slot-ratio 4 --out <out>.md         # the second selector
node scripts/r3-crops.ts --in <out>.json --out <dir>           # OWN-SIDE crops, outlined
node scripts/r3-score.ts --in <candidates>.json --labels <labels>.json
```

The measured candidate sets are frozen beside their labels
(`r3-sweep-2026-09-16.candidates.json`, `.slot-candidates.json`) so the scoring reproduces
forever — a fresh sweep no longer yields the same 92, because 13 of them no longer exist (below).

### How a label was decided, and the one place it is not independent

`packageForModel` crops both PNGs at `implBox ?? designBox` — the same region from both sides,
which is right for a finding about a pair and shows nothing when the pair is 1000 px apart. So
`scripts/r3-crops.ts` crops **each element where it sits on its own side**, with enough margin to
show the row it is in, and outlines it. That is the evidence a label was decided on.

| `how` | n (γ set) | what it means |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| `pixels` | 17 | read off the two PNGs, independent of every feature under test |
| `family` | 4 | pixels on a sibling candidate of identical shape, named in the label |
| `detail` | 64 | the strings, the element's own style, and the texts sharing its line on its own side |
| `prior` | 7 | the plan's existing 7 labels, re-checked here |

**The honest limit, stated rather than hidden:** a `detail` label reads an element's line-mates and
a containment rule reads its container. Both are "local surroundings", so a containment rule scored
against a `detail` label is not fully independent of it. The scorer therefore prints the
pixels-only subset as its own table, and containment is refuted on **both**.

`DEFAULT_MAX_GAMMA` is 100 and geometric pairing cannot exceed it. Step 5's first bullet ("below
the floor, do not form geometric pairs at all") therefore addresses a population that is *already
distance-bounded*, while every long-distance pairing in the corpus is text or slot.

### A γ ceiling is refuted again, and harder than before

The plan's refutation had correct pairs reaching γ 873 and wrong ones starting at 686 — an overlap
of 187. The sweep widens it to **the entire candidate band**: the longest pair in the corpus,
`docs-accountant-mobile`'s `Požiadať o doklad` at **γ 1261 — Δ (−114, −1132)** — is CORRECT (one
dark CTA per side; the comp puts it in a bottom sticky bar, the implementation under the client
header), while `client-pending-accountant-mobile`'s mis-pairing sits at **γ 210**.

| γ ceiling | wrong caught (of 12) | correct broken (of 79) |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 1000 | 1 | 1 |
| 700 | 4 | 6 |
| 500 | 5 | 10 |

Every row costs more than it buys. The same holds for both axis readings of "correct long pairs
keep their x": `|Δx| > 100` catches 5 and breaks 12; `|Δx| > 60 ∧ |Δy| > 60` catches 5 and breaks
10.

### Containment — REFUTED in all three readings

The plan's wording ("correct long pairs keep their x and their local structure; wrong ones move on
both axes and change role") does not say which comparison it means, so all three were scored, using
the shipped per-side placement (`containersOf` + `groupByRegion` at `minGroup: 1`, the same call
`groupUnmatched` makes).

| reading | caught (of 12) | broken (of 79) | pixels-only: caught (7) / broken (9) |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| A — containers disagree in SHAPE | 3 | 18 | 3 / 1 |
| B — A, and an unplaced side counts as disagreement | 11 | 63 | 7 / 9 |
| C — A, or the element sits at a different offset inside it | 4 | 18 | 3 / 1 |

**The reason is the map's own miss rate, one level down.** The container column is ABSENT exactly
where the decision is needed: only **4 of the 12 wrong pairs have both sides placed**, against 34
of 79 correct. `reconcile.md` §R2 already says a placement report states its own miss rate and that
a pair the map places nothing on is read differently — this is that limit reappearing as a feature.

And where containment IS available it still fails. `refdiff-compare-desktop-ghost` pairs a 252×31
annotation sentence with a 7×14 red count badge **inside the same container on both sides**
(`(1039, 86) 321×734`) — a mis-pairing that every containment reading keeps.

### What the 79 correct pairs are, and why they matter

They are not noise to be tolerated: a long-γ correct pair is refdiff reporting something true and
useful — *the comp's bottom CTA is in the implementation's header*, *the comp right-aligns the
message author and the implementation left-aligns it* (γ 448, Δx −422), *the same library row is
higher up a shorter list* (γ 873, Δx 0), *the confidence percentage moved onto its own line in the
transaction row* (γ 238). **Any distance rule buys a handful of refusals by destroying these.**

### The complement of the rule's own list — audited, because that is what R3 is

A rule is a list-shaped report and absence from it is not evidence of correctness. Of the **58 slot
pairs in the corpus**, the ceiling at ratio 4 refuses 15 and KEEPS 43 — and **22 of those 43 carry
token-DISJOINT texts**, among them `ZÚČT. OBDOBIE` ↔ `Aplikácia · nahral(a) Test Owner` (ratio 3.60,
the same label-vs-value defect as the pair above it), `Návrh 96 %` ↔ `Zatiaľ nemáte žiadne
dokumenty.` (3.04) and `Automatické priradenie nových klientov` ↔ `PRIPRAVUJEME` (2.86).

**So the ceiling's PRECISION is measured and its RECALL is not.** The slot family is larger than
the 13 pairs a ratio ceiling reaches, and the rest of it is open work, not a closed question. Also
worth recording: `unrelatedPairing` IS applied in pass 3, and it fires on almost none of these,
because its third clause needs each text to have a same-text counterpart on the other side — the
same structural blindness the plan opened with, one pass over.

## What shipped, and what it cost

`DEFAULT_SLOT_MAX_AREA_RATIO = 5` in `structural/match.ts` — a slot may STRETCH, which is the
pass's whole premise, but it is still the same slot and not a thirtyfold different thing. 5 is the
gap between the two populations above, read off the corpus rather than chosen. The refusal is
pushed to `vetoed` like the existing veto, because a refusal a reader cannot see is the same defect
as a suppression they cannot see.

**Measured on all three corpora, re-captured end to end** (the before-picture is this file's
sibling `baseline-matching-2026-09-16.md` at commit `6025583`; the after is the same path in this
change):

| corpus | matched | via slot | via text | via geometry | designOnly | implOnly | vetoed | findings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| refdiff (9) | 1556 → **1555** | 16 → 15 | 746 → 746 | 794 → 794 | 1265 → 1266 | 272 → 273 | 67 → 69 | 2232 → 2233 |
| uctoinak2 (29) | 747 → **737** | 19 → 9 | 331 → 331 | 397 → 397 | 1096 → 1106 | 672 → 682 | 3 → 14 | 3652 → 3627 |
| uctoinak2-storybook (14) | 700 → **698** | 23 → 21 | 347 → 347 | 330 → 330 | 136 → 138 | 80 → 82 | 0 → 2 | 1317 → 1309 |

- **−13 matched of 3003, every one of them `via: "slot"`, one per pair on 13 pairs.**
  `matchedVia.text` and `matchedVia.geometry` are unmoved on all 52 pairs, and the other 39 pairs
  are `+0 / −0`.
- **Findings 7201 → 7169.** On the two `doc-detail-*-mobile` pairs the delta is **`+2 / −6`** each:
  six confident findings about a field label and a field value replaced by one honest
  `missing-element` and one `extra-element`. That is the outcome step 5 exists for, measured on the
  corpus's best pairs.
- `refdiff-compare-desktop-ghost` is the one pair that gains a finding (`+1 / −0`): its new
  `missing-element` is already named by that pair's own ignore policy, so only the `extra-element`
  surfaces. The mis-pairing had been producing **no findings at all** — the data-slot policy
  classified its text difference as expected — so it was consuming one element from each side in
  total silence. That is the worse failure mode, and the one a finding count can never show.

### The one consequence that is not cosmetic

**`client-pending-accountant-mobile` moves from `polish` to `reconcile`**: its match rate falls
0.72 → 0.69, across the 0.70 floor. It was the corpus's nearest-the-floor `polish` pair, and the
pairing it lost was the pixel-confirmed `Všetko` ↔ client-subtitle mis-pairing — so its true
correspondence was always 25 of 36 and the 26th was a fiction. The label moving is the phase signal
receiving a truer input. But the general shape must be stated plainly: **refusing a pair lowers the
match rate, and any pair within one pairing of the floor will cross it.** Corpus phase totals go
28P/24R → 27P/25R.

`tx-picker-accountant-desktop` shows `+1 / −1 R1` on its own: the corpus's one known
non-deterministic element (a flapping `boxShadow` ring token), not this change — its `matching` row
is identical.

### The acceptance criterion, restated

Step 5's written criterion was *"no pair at confidence ≥ 0.5 changes by a single finding"*. The
measurement disqualifies it: four of the thirteen refusals are on `polish` pairs, two of them the
best-corresponding pairs in the corpus, and every one of those four is a labelled mis-pairing. A
criterion that forbids them forbids the only change the evidence supports.

**Restated: no pair loses a pairing the labelled set calls CORRECT, and the guard is the labelled
set, not the finding count.** Met: 13 refusals, each individually labelled — 12 `wrong`, 1
`leafshape`, **0 `correct`** — and `matchedVia.text` / `matchedVia.geometry` unmoved on all 52
pairs. Re-running the sweep against the post-change corpus reproduces all 52 `matching` blocks and
returns 85 γ-candidates where there were 92: the seven that vanished are six labelled `wrong` plus
the one `leafshape`, and **no labelled-correct candidate left the list**.

## What this leaves open

- **The slot family's true size.** 22 of the 43 slot pairs the ceiling keeps are token-disjoint and
  several are visibly wrong. Recall is unmeasured; a labelled census of all 58 is the next step.
- **The `via: "text"` family is untouched and still real** — `Vybavené` on four Messages pairs,
  `Nahrať doklad` on `messages-owner-desktop` (γ 778: the comp's in-thread action button against
  the implementation's global top-nav upload button). Five confirmed mis-pairings among 79 correct
  long-γ text pairs, with **no feature tested here separating them**. Containment, distance and
  axis decomposition are all refuted; whatever the discriminator is, this sweep did not find it,
  and a text pair's ~94% correctness rate in this band is the bar any future rule must beat.
- **Step 1's `unverified` exemption for `via: "text"` still stands unexamined** in light of that
  5-of-79 error rate.
